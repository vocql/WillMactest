Before you punish, ask four things

  • Am I calm? Never punish while mad.
  • Did I check the logs? Facts first.
  • Did I give a reason? Always.
  • Did I log it? Every warning, mute and ban.

Strike system

An optional alternative to warnings. Every strike is logged, and the fifth is the last.

  1. 1 strikeWarningTalk to them. Nothing else yet.
  2. 2 strikesShort mute10 min to 3 hours.
  3. 3 strikesLong mute12 hours to 3 days.
  4. 4 strikesKickFinal warning. They can rejoin.
  5. 5 strikesBanThe end of the line.
Instant actions skip strikes. Raiding, doxxing and spam bots are banned straight away, and extreme harassment is a temp or perma ban.

Log everything

Log every warning, strike, mute, kick and ban. It helps staff stay consistent and gives appeals a record to check.

How appeals work

Anyone can appeal a punishment: ban, mute, kick or warning. A panel votes. Nobody decides alone.

  1. PunishedBan, mute, kick or warning
  2. Wait 24hCool-off period
  3. Appeal sentWebsite form
  4. 2-3 staff reviewCheck the evidence
  5. VoteAccept or deny
  6. DecisionMessage sent

Wait 24 hours

At least 24 hours must pass after the punishment.

Only one appeal

One appeal at a time. No duplicates.

Be respectful

No insults. Insults mean an instant denial.

What every appeal answers

  1. Discord username + ID
  2. What were you punished for?
  3. Do you understand what you did wrong?
  4. Why should it be lifted?
  5. What will you do differently?
  6. Anything else you want to add? (optional)

Appeal page to share with members: voqcl.com/willmacgta/appeal

Decisions

How staff review an appeal and what happens next.

Staff review

  • 2-3 staff members review every appeal.
  • The person who issued the punishment never decides alone.
  • They should recuse themselves from the vote.

Accepted

  • Punishment is lifted (unbanned if it was a ban).
  • Put them on probation for 1-2 weeks.
  • Any rule break during probation means an instant ban.

Denied

  • Send a short reason, like “Appeal denied due to lack of accountability.”
  • Optional: allow a retry after 7-14 days.

Auto-deny cases

Don’t even consider appeals for these.

RaidingDoxxingThreatsHate speech

Appeal rules for staff

How to handle an appeal from the moment someone asks about it to the final decision.

  1. Point members to the appeal page.

    Don’t debate punishments in chat or DMs. Send them here instead: https://voqcl.com/willmacgta/appeal/

  2. Keep appeals out of DMs.

    Never discuss an appeal privately with the member.

  3. Don’t judge your own punishments.

    If you issued it, leave the appeal to another staff member.

  4. Check their history first.

    Use Load from Discord via ViceGuard and User Lookup before making a decision.

  5. Give a clear reason.

    A reason is required, and members can see final reasons. “No” or “ok” doesn’t count.

  6. Ask instead of guessing.

    Missing details? Use Ask for more info. The member gets a DM and can reply on the appeal page.

  7. Escalate serious cases.

    Threats, doxxing, NSFW, raids, alt accounts or anything you’re unsure about should go to the admins.

  8. Keep notes private.

    Members never see staff notes. Don’t share dashboard screenshots outside the staff team.

  9. Stay active.

    Going to be away? Let @voqcl know.

  10. Protect your account.

    Turn on 2-Factor Authentication and never share your login. If your account is compromised, tell a Mac Lead right away.

Good to know

  • Members can only have one open appeal at a time. If denied, they must wait 7 days before appealing again.
  • @ViceGuard can only DM members who share a server with it. If a DM fails, the dashboard will show it, and the result still appears on their appeal page.
  • If an unban or timeout removal fails, an admin can press Retry. It never applies the action twice.
